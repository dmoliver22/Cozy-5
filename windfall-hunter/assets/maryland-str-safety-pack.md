Do not use the Maryland seal or the words "official" or "state-approved" anywhere.

## What the law requires of a host (plain English)

Every dwelling rented for fewer than 30 days must:
1. Post an evacuation diagram, in a conspicuous place, showing every exit from the unit (and the building's exits if the unit is inside a larger building).
2. Post a list of emergency telephone numbers.
3. Provide a working fire extinguisher.
4. Have working smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarms.

Booking platforms must notify hosts of these duties, which is why hosts will be searching for a ready-made pack this autumn.

## Files in the pack (make each in Canva, US Letter, editable)

### File 1: Evacuation diagram (one page)
Header: EMERGENCY EXIT PLAN / PLAN DE EVACUACIÓN
Sub-header (small): Posted in compliance with Maryland's Short-Term Rental Fire Safety Act (Chapter 9 of 2026), effective October 1, 2026.
Body: a blank floor-plan grid (10 x 10 light squares) with a legend the host fills in:
- YOU ARE HERE (star marker)
- EXIT (green arrow markers, at least two)
- STAIRS, WINDOW EXIT, FIRE EXTINGUISHER (icons)
- Building exits (for apartments and condos): a second smaller grid labelled "Building exits from this unit"
Footer line: "In a fire, leave immediately. Do not use elevators. Meet at: ____________ (meeting point)."
Design note: give the host a second version with a 12 x 8 grid for wide units, and a blank one with no grid for hand-drawing. Include a one-paragraph instruction: "Draw your unit's walls, doors and windows. Mark every door to the outside and every window a person could climb through. Mark where the extinguisher is. Post this where guests enter."

### File 2: Emergency numbers sign (one page)
Header: EMERGENCY NUMBERS
Rows (large type, blanks for the host to fill):
- Emergency (fire, police, medical): 911
- Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222
- Local fire and rescue (non-emergency): ____________
- Local police (non-emergency): ____________
- Nearest hospital (name and address): ____________
- Property address (for guests to read to dispatchers): ____________
- Host or property manager: ____________
- Gas or electric utility emergency line: ____________
Footer: "Posted in compliance with Maryland's Short-Term Rental Fire Safety Act, effective October 1, 2026."

### File 3: Safety checklist and record (one page)
Checklist with date and initials columns, to be kept by the host:
- Evacuation diagram posted at the entry (date, initials)
- Emergency numbers posted (date, initials)
- Fire extinguisher present, charged, accessible; tag date: ______
- Smoke alarms: one per sleeping area and level; test date: ______
- Carbon monoxide alarms: on each level with fuel-burning appliances or an attached garage; test date: ______
- Batteries replaced: ______
Footer note: "Keep this record. Booking platforms may ask you to confirm compliance."

### File 4: Compliance attestation (one page, for platform upload)
Title: Short-Term Rental Fire Safety Compliance Statement
"I, ____________, the host of the dwelling at ____________, Maryland, confirm that as of ____________ the unit has: (1) a posted evacuation diagram showing all exits; (2) a posted list of emergency telephone numbers; (3) a working fire extinguisher; (4) working smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarms, as required by Maryland's Short-Term Rental Fire Safety Act (Chapter 9 of 2026, effective October 1, 2026)."
Signature and date lines.
Small print: "This statement is prepared by the host. It is not issued by the State of Maryland or any county."
