Made the day Apple announced HomeView. Files: `homeview-counter-cradle.scad` (parametric source, edit the numbers at the top) and `homeview-counter-cradle-v0.stl` (loose fit, prints as-is, no supports, 160 x 60 x 51 mm; the STL lives in the GitHub repo under windfall-hunter/assets, or render it from the .scad in OpenSCAD).

## What it is
A counter or shelf cradle for the **Apple HomeView (Wall Mounted)** unit, the $299 model that ships without a stand and needs one of Schneider's $60 to $130 magnetic wall mounts to work. This cradle stands it upright on a counter, powered by its own USB-C cable through the notch in the back lip, for people who do not want to put it on a wall. Built from Apple's published numbers only: 145.5 x 145.5 x 9.8 mm, 286 g.

## Description
Stand your Apple HomeView (Wall Mounted) on a counter, desk or shelf instead of a wall. This cradle holds the 5.7-inch unit upright with a 1 mm clearance slot, a tall back lip, and a notch for the USB-C cable so it runs straight out the back. Prints flat with no supports in about 2 hours on a standard printer (PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 15 percent infill).

Made from Apple's published dimensions (145.5 x 145.5 x 9.8 mm). This is version 0, a loose fit; a tightened version will follow once units ship on Nov 13 2026. The parametric source is included so you can change the clearance, lip heights and notch width yourself.
